My Favorite Foods
Fried Chicken
A popular dish made by seasoning chicken, coating it in flour or breadcrumbs, then deep-frying it until golden and crispy on the outside while juicy on the inside.
Adobo
A classic Filipino dish where meat (usually chicken or pork) is marinated and simmered in a mix of soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns, giving it a savory and slightly tangy flavor.
Kaldareta
A hearty Filipino stew usually made with beef, cooked slowly in tomato sauce with potatoes, carrots, bell peppers, and sometimes liver spread or cheese for added richness and flavor.
